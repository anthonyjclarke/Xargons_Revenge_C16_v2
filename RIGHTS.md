# Rights and Preservation

## Historical Software

The Xargon's Revenge source carries the notice "(C) MICRO PROJECTS 1985", and its scrolling message says "Xargon's Revenge © Micro Projects 1985". The game was published by Gremlin Graphics Software Ltd., whose inlay says "© 1985 … All rights reserved." These notices are kept in the preserved files. The game binary and the historical source keep whatever rights status they already had.

The source disk also holds third-party tools from the period: the Commodore 64 editor, © 1982 Commodore Business Machines, and "DOS V5.2 (C) Bugga-Soft 1984". They're kept because they were on the disk, not because they're part of the game, and their rights are unchanged.

---

## Printed Material and Artwork

The cassette inlay, cover painting, gameplay screenshots, title picture and *C16/Plus 4 Classics II* leaflet stay with their respective rights holders. The leaflet also describes Blagger (© 1985 Aligata Software Ltd.), Monkey Magic (© 1985 Solar Software Ltd.) and Timeslip (© 1985 English Software Company), which belong to their own publishers. The [artifact catalogue](docs/ARTIFACTS.md) lists what I've included.

---

## Repository Status

I've set this repository up for studying, reassembling and preserving the historical material. I haven't assigned any new open-source or artwork licence, and making the repository public doesn't grant any rights beyond a licence that is expressly supplied.

The build scripts adapt the approach from my [Dorks Dilemma C16 repository](https://github.com/anthonyjclarke/Dorks_Dilemma_C16), and the handbook uses [Marked](https://marked.js.org/) and [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/) under the licences kept in `scripts/vendor/`. The build never rewrites the original input files.
